# Quickstart

To send an existing private balance, open the dashboard's **Send** button, enter a
registered `@username`, amount in USDC, and optional note. Review and confirm.
Reopen a pending transfer from the dashboard or History after reload; unlock and
Continue if it is still preparing. Only active-pool balance funds direct sends.

Get set up in a few minutes. No seed phrases, no gas tokens to buy.

## 1. Create your account

Open Mawee and sign in with a **passkey** — the same fingerprint or face unlock
your phone or laptop already uses. No seed phrase to write down or lose.

## 2. Claim your username

Pick a username, like `@dinar`. This is what you'll share with people instead of
a long wallet address. Your payment link becomes something clean like:

```text
mawee/pay/dinar
```

## 3. Set your PIN

Choose a 6-digit PIN. It's your backup key — it helps you recover your account
and unlock spending. Keep it somewhere safe.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Mawee will **never** ask you for your PIN over email, chat, or DM. If someone
does, it's a scam.
{% endhint %}

## 4. Share and get paid

Share your link or QR code. When someone pays, the money lands in your **private
balance** — visible only to you.

* Want a fixed price and a label (e.g. "Logo design — $300")? Create a
  **payment link** for it.
* Prefer in-person? Show your **QR code**.

## 5. Cash out whenever

Withdraw your balance to any Monad wallet (`0x…`). Network fees are covered by
Mawee.

---

That's the whole loop: **claim → share → get paid → cash out.** Everything in
between stays private automatically.
